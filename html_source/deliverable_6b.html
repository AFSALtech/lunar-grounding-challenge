RSK-E01 Electrical Pre-Contact Vacuum Dust Arcing
Failure Mode: High-voltage spark breakdown jumps across open gap between probe tip and lander before full mating.		 Suspended charged lunar regolith dust lowers dielectric breakdown threshold in lunar vacuum. 5 × 4 = 20 Deeply recessed contact pin inside dielectric sleeve + mechanical sequence interlock switch. Circuit remains physically open during insertion stroke and only engages after full mechanical mating eliminates the open gap. 2 × 1 = 2 High-voltage chamber test (120 kV, 10^-6 Torr) with JSC-1A lunar regolith simulant dust cloud.
RSK-E02 Electrical Resistor String Dielectric Flashover
Failure Mode: Surface flashover across 1.0 GΩ cermet resistor string body causes low-impedance short circuit.		 Thermal expansion mismatch micro-cracks or trapped gas voids in potting under deep vacuum. 5 × 3 = 15 Segmented 3-resistor cermet string with ceramic creepage barriers; vacuum centrifugal centrifugal degassing and potting with space-grade NuSil CV-1142; rated to 150 kV pulse withstand (50% derating). 3 × 1 = 3 Partial discharge testing (IEC 60270) and dielectric withstand test at 150 kV DC across thermal range.
RSK-M01 Mechanical / Dust Regolith Jamming of Docking Funnel
Failure Mode: Accumulated abrasive lunar dust inside guide sleeve binds or prevents full seating of EVA probe wand.		 Sub-micron regolith particles (0.02 - 10 µm) kicked up by boots entering open receptacle. 4 × 4 = 16 Dual PTFE spring-energized scraper seals + high-gradient NdFeB magnetic dust collar at funnel throat to trap magnetic/agglutinate particles; 60° self-clearing funnel cone with MoS2 dry lubricant. 2 × 2 = 4 Life cycle insertion test (50 cycles) in dirty vacuum chamber with sub-micron abrasive simulant.
RSK-T01 Thermal Cryogenic Freezing in Lunar PSR
Failure Mode: Extreme temperatures down to -361°F (55 K) cause embrittlement, contact stiffness, or excessive resistor TCR shift.		 Absence of solar radiation in permanently shadowed crater floors during Artemis IV EVAs. 4 × 3 = 12 15-layer MLI blanket around resistor core; dual redundant 12W Kapton strip heaters drawing from lander 28V DC bus; core temperature maintained above -40°C; low TCR cermet (<50 ppm/K). 2 × 1 = 2 Thermal vacuum chamber (TVAC) cycling test between 55 K and 300 K with active heater loop.
RSK-H01 Human Factors Premature Disengagement by Crew
Failure Mode: Astronaut pulls EVA wand out of receptacle before full charge dissipation (<2 seconds), causing residual shock.		 Crew hurry, fatigue, or low visibility in pitch-black shadow conditions. 4 × 3 = 12 Spring-loaded mechanical capture detent requires deliberate 35 N unlatching pull force; high-contrast tri-color LED status ring with solid green 'SAFE TO DISENGAGE' visual confirmation. 2 × 1 = 2 Human-in-the-loop pressurized suit glove ergonomic evaluation in NBL / simulated low-gravity mock-up.
RSK-O01 Operational Cable Snag or Entanglement During Ingress
Failure Mode: EVA grounding cable snags on lander ladder rungs, causing trip hazard or damaged cable.		 Slack in grounding tether during crew movement up the ladder. 3 × 3 = 9 Spring-retracted cable reel mounted on suit harness keeps cable taut (<5 N tension); auto-stows wand into harness holster upon release with quick-breakaway safety link (45 N). 2 × 1 = 2 Ladder ascent mobility trials with pressurized suit harness.
RSK-E03 Electrical Arc Plasma Leakage to Exterior Environment
Failure Mode: Internal micro-discharge vents plasma or UV photons that degrade outer suit garment.		 Contact bounce during mechanical switch closure inside chamber. 5 × 2 = 10 Sealed OFHC Copper-Nickel Faraday cage surrounding switch contacts with tortuous optical labyrinth vents; located on lander ladder rail >1.5 m standoff distance from suit fabrics. 1 × 1 = 1 Optical emission spectroscopy and streak camera imaging during 120 kV switch closure in vacuum.
