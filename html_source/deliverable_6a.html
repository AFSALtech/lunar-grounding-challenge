A-GATE: Risk Assessment & Fault-Tolerance Methodology

FMECA Framework, Redundancy Pathways, and Environmental Safety Assurance for Artemis EVAs

1. Risk Management Framework & Philosophy

The A-GATE system safety architecture adheres to NASA-STD-8729.1 (Reliability and Maintainability) and NPR 8705.4 (Risk Classification for NASA Payloads). A comprehensive Failure Modes, Effects, and Criticality Analysis (FMECA) was performed across all flight phases (launch, transit, lunar surface deployment, and multi-EVA operations). The accompanying Risk Matrix (Deliverable 6B) identifies seven primary operational, environmental, and human factors risks, detailing specific design mitigations that eliminate single-point failures.

2. Dual-Fault Tolerance & Redundancy Architecture

Electrical & Arc Suppression Redundancy
  • Passive Primary Discharge: Charge dissipation relies 100% on passive 1.0 GΩ thick-film cermet resistors. Even under complete loss of lander power, the astronaut is safely discharged in 2.0 s.
  • Triple Arc Containment Barrier: (1) Recessed pin inside 99.5% alumina sleeve; (2) Sequenced zero-gap mechanical interlock; (3) Sealed OFHC copper Faraday cage (>60 dB EMI shielding).
  • Creepage Derating: 150 kV DC rated insulation provides 50% voltage margin over the -100 kV potential.
Environmental & Dust Exclusion Redundancy
  • Dual Scraper Seals: Virgin PTFE wiper rings with Elgiloy cantilever springs mechanically shear regolith dust coats.
  • Magnetic Trap Collar: 0.45 T NdFeB permanent magnetic ring strips ferromagnetic agglutinate nanoparticles at throat.
  • Self-Wiping BeCu Contacts: 12 N wiping contact scrubs gold mating surfaces clean upon every stroke.
  • Dry Lubrication: Bonded MoS2 / DLC coating (MIL-PRF-81329) prevents cold-welding and binding across 55 K to 238 K.

4. Human Factors & Multi-EVA Operability Assurance

In accordance with NASA-STD-3001, A-GATE mitigates operational error by designing out cognitive and dexterity barriers:

  • Tritium & Luminescent Alignment: Self-illuminating crosshairs provide visual guidance even in pitch-black shadow craters without helmet spotlight reflection wash.
  • One-Handed Blind Operation: Large 160 mm entry cone captures wand with ±30° misalignment; crew can stabilize with opposite hand.
  • Multi-EVA Reusability: 100+ cycle rated life with zero consumables replacement guarantees full support for all 4 EVAs over 10 days.
